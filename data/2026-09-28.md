### [1] [Informational and algebraic renormalization group](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.30362)
*Takato Mori,Teruaki Nagasawa*

Main category: hep-th

TL;DR: The paper reformulates Wilsonian RG as a quantum channel (with Kraus operators giving pure conditional trajectories), extends it algebraically beyond momentum-space factorized settings via a conditional expectation onto a low-energy algebra built from the one-particle spectrum, and constructs a resource theory of RG whose relative-entropy monotone near a 2D IR fixed point scales as c - c_IR to quadratic order; a complementary monotone quantifies discarded UV information.


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: Renormalization group (RG) is a core concept spanning statistical mechanics to quantum field theory, but its schemes differ widely between field theory and many-body physics. The authors seek a unified, information-theoretic formulation of Wilsonian RG that bridges these settings and applies beyond the usual momentum-space, factorized framework.

Method: Wilsonian RG is formulated as a quantum channel, whose Kraus representation yields pure conditional trajectories for pure inputs and recovers mixed-state flows upon averaging. An algebraic extension is developed for settings beyond momentum-space factorized ones: the low-energy algebra is constructed from the one-particle spectrum, and coarse graining is implemented as a conditional expectation onto it. A resource theory of RG is then built with fixed points as free states, yielding a relative-entropy monotone. A complementary measure of discarded UV information is also introduced.

Result: Under stated assumptions, the relative-entropy monotone is proportional to c - c_IR to quadratic order along a single stable RG direction near a two-dimensional IR fixed point. The complementary UV-information measure is shown to be monotone under successive coarse-graining maps, and its holographic realization is discussed.

Conclusion: The work provides a quantum-information framework for Wilsonian RG: RG as a quantum channel, an algebraic coarse-graining via conditional expectation, and a resource theory whose relative-entropy monotone exhibits the expected c - c_IR behavior near a 2D IR fixed point. The complementary UV-information monotone admits a holographic realization, suggesting connections between RG irreversibility and holography.

Abstract: Renormalization group (RG) is a core concept in physics from statistical mechanics to quantum field theory, yet its schemes differ widely between field theory and many-body physics. We formulate the Wilsonian RG as a quantum channel, whose Kraus representation yields pure conditional trajectories for pure inputs and recovers mixed-state flows upon averaging. We then develop an algebraic extension applicable beyond the usual momentum-space, factorized setting: the low-energy algebra is constructed from the one-particle spectrum, and coarse graining is a conditional expectation onto it. We further construct a resource theory of RG whose free states are fixed points. Under stated assumptions, the resulting relative-entropy monotone is proportional to $c-c_{\rm IR}$ to quadratic order along a single stable RG direction near a two-dimensional IR fixed point. We also introduce a complementary measure of the UV information discarded by coarse graining, establish its monotonicity for successive coarse-graining maps, and discuss its holographic realization.

</details>


### [2] [de Sitter Spacetime: Geometry, Causal Structure, and Quantum Fields](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.30453)
*Azadeh Maleknejad,Ethan Richardson*

Main category: hep-th

TL;DR: Lecture notes introducing de Sitter spacetime—its geometry, symmetries, quantum fields, and perturbations on a fixed 3+1-dimensional background—with emphasis on physical interpretation and cosmological applications.


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: de Sitter spacetime is central to modern cosmology, approximating both inflation and the present accelerated expansion. It provides a simple yet distinctive setting where gravity, spacetime symmetry, and quantum field theory deviate qualitatively from Minkowski-space counterparts.

Method: Pedagogical lecture notes covering coordinate systems, causal structure, unitary representations, particle production in time-dependent backgrounds, and cosmological perturbations.

Result: A coherent exposition of quantum fields and perturbations on a fixed de Sitter background, highlighting how curvature reshapes notions of mass, vacuum, particles, and late-time observables.

Conclusion: The notes deliver a physically interpreted, cosmology-oriented account of de Sitter spacetime, bridging its geometric and symmetry structure with quantum field theory and perturbation theory.

Abstract: de~Sitter spacetime plays a central role in modern cosmology, providing an excellent approximation to both inflation and the present accelerated expansion. It also offers a particularly simple setting in which gravity, spacetime symmetry, and quantum field theory depart qualitatively from their Minkowski-space counterparts. These lecture notes introduce de Sitter spacetime, from its geometry and symmetries to quantum fields and perturbations on a fixed \(3+1\)-dimensional de Sitter background. We cover coordinate systems, causal structure, unitary representations, particle production in time-dependent backgrounds, and cosmological perturbations, with emphasis on physical interpretation, applications to cosmology, and how curvature reshapes the notions of mass, vacuum, particles, and late-time observables.

</details>
